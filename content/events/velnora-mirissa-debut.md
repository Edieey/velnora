VELNORA – Mirissa Debut marks a historic milestone as the first-ever international music festival created by a Maldivian brand to be hosted in Mirissa, Sri Lanka. Set against the stunning beaches of Mirissa, this one-day electronic music experience will bring together internationally acclaimed artists, leading DJs from the Maldives and Sri Lanka, immersive stage production, breathtaking visuals, and an unforgettable atmosphere. More than just a festival, VELNORA is a celebration of music, culture, and the connection between two island nations, creating a new destination for electronic music lovers across the region.
